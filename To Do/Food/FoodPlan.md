# Mukul's food plan

Source: Prachi Pandit (Qualia nutrition), "Mukul_and Garima 4 week meal plan - Oct 2026" (shared 12 Oct 2026), plus his stated preferences from the wiki page "Nutrition Preferences" (updated 30 Sep 2026). To refresh, replace this file with the next plan; the app reads it at runtime.

## Where he is (October check-in)
- Goals, in order: fewer energy drops, less bloating, better recovery from running, padel and cricket.
- Weight steady at ~66.6 kg. The plan eats at maintenance; nothing to chase.
- Week: three gym days, one zone-2 run, padel Saturday, cricket Sunday. Trains fasted.

## Daily targets
- Protein at least 90 g every day, rest days included. Rough split: breakfast ~25 g, lunch ~30 g, 3:45 snack ~15 g, dinner ~25 g.
- Fibre about 40 g a day from cooked vegetables, oats, millets, moong/masoor and reheated rice. Cooked vegetables one cup per meal.
- Meal times: breakfast 9:30, lunch 1:00 (packed lunch reheats in the office microwave), snack 3:45, dinner 7:00. Kitchen closed after dinner for 12+ hours.
- Dinner always includes ½ cup cooled-and-reheated rice (resistant starch).

## Keep out (always)
Poha · cashews (including cashew paste in gravies and bhapa) · maida/white flour (bakery bread, naan, pav, biscuits) · baingan (brinjal) · bell peppers of any colour · karela (bitter gourd) · mushrooms · chilli · raw garlic · papaya · beer · inulin, chicory and prebiotic powders · rava/suji · dosa (idli yes, dosa no).
Raw food: cooked only, no salads or kachumbar for now.
Legumes still out: chana, chole, lobia, sattu, edamame, soya chunks.

## Keep small
- Besan: a spoon as a binder only. No besan chilla, gatte or kadhi. Never besan and beans in the same meal.
- Dairy: curd, chaas, Greek yogurt, skyr and paneer are in, up to 2 servings a day (whey not counted). No glass of milk or milk-heavy sweets (use lactose-free milk). Never pair dairy with sugar or honey.
- Rajma: only twice across four weeks, ¾ cup, soaked and pressure-cooked soft. (He likes rajma, but this cap wins.)

## Balance rules across a week
- No lunch or dinner repeats within a week. Do not serve the same dish two days running.
- Paneer on no more than 3 days a week. Firm tofu 3–4 meals a week.
- Gluten: 2–3 meals a week, no more, at lunch rather than dinner; khapli atta (emmer) rotis or whole-wheat sourdough. A slice of true sourdough at breakfast does not count.
- Grains mostly gluten-free: jowar, ragi, foxtail millet, quinoa, oats, rice.
- Protein rotates: eggs, whey, moong, masoor, toor dal, firm tofu, paneer, yogurt. Eggs are not every morning (Qualia flagged eggs-most-mornings as monotony).
- Each week cooks around 4 core vegetables (listed per week below); across a week aim for at least 4–5 different vegetables to widen gut bacteria.
- Fruit (kiwi, firm banana, stewed apple) before 4 PM only.
- Sweets only after a meal, ideally after lunch, never late evening. Lactose-free home-made kulfi and milk desserts are fine as one portion after lunch.

## His stated preferences
- Eggs only ever go with toast (sourdough). Omelette, boiled or scrambled is fine, partnered with toast. Do NOT pair egg with chilla, dosa or upma unless the plan explicitly does.
- No egg at dinner as an omelette. (Egg curry and bhurji at dinner appear in Prachi's plan and are fine.)
- Tuesday is a no-egg day: Tuesday breakfast is a no-egg grain option.
- Liked: cheese toast + scrambled egg; home-made khao suey ("highly enjoyable"); matar paneer; dal fry; rajma (capped above).
- Weekday food has felt "functional, with indifference". He wants variety and genuinely enjoyable options: themes, flavour boosters (tadkas, chutneys, Bengali, South Indian, Asian-style tofu), not the same three dals.

## After exercise
After a long run, padel, cricket or a hard session, within an hour: recovery drink (250 ml lactose-free milk, firm banana, 2 tsp cacao, pinch of salt) or a recovery snack (rice cakes + almond butter; dahi chawal + 2 boiled eggs; half a sweet potato + 2 boiled eggs; sourdough + almond butter or egg).
